# Automated Candidate Sourcing System
## Finance and Operations Accountant — Swivel Group (Sri Lanka Market)

**Role:** Finance and Operations Accountant | Full-time contract | Hybrid | Australian shift (6:30 AM–3:30 PM IST) | Australian leave calendar

**Design principle:** AI and automation accelerate *discovery, extraction, scoring and drafting*. A human recruiter makes every decision that affects a candidate — who gets contacted, who advances, and who is rejected. No step in this system auto-contacts or auto-rejects a person.

## 1. Ideal Candidate Persona

### Likely current/previous job titles
- Bookkeeper / Senior Bookkeeper
- Client Accountant / Client Bookkeeper (common in AU-facing BPOs)
- Xero Specialist / Xero Certified Advisor
- Accounts Officer / Accounts Executive
- Finance and Operations Accountant / Finance Executive
- Management Accountant (part-qualified)
- Assistant Accountant / Accountant
- Offshore Accountant / Process Associate – Finance & Accounting (F&A BPO title)
- Audit Associate / Semi-Senior Auditor transitioning into bookkeeping/BPO (Big 4 / mid-tier trained)
- Accounts Payable / Accounts Receivable Executive (with Xero exposure)

### Relevant industries and company types
- Offshore/outsourced accounting and bookkeeping providers serving Australian clients (BPO/KPO finance delivery centres)
- Australian accounting/tax firms with Sri Lanka delivery hubs or remote staff
- Big 4 and mid-tier audit/advisory firms (KPMG, EY, PwC, Deloitte, BDO, Baker Tilly, Nexia, Crowe, Grant Thornton — Sri Lanka offices, particularly Global Delivery/GDS teams)
- Xero-partner bookkeeping practices in Sri Lanka
- Manufacturing, printing, freight/logistics, or distribution companies (for job-costing/inventory exposure — direct match to Swivel's environment)
- Shared services/GBS centres of multinational companies with ANZ-facing finance processes

*Note: specific firm names change roster frequently — verify current staff via LinkedIn company search and the Xero Advisor Directory (filter: Sri Lanka) rather than relying on a static list.*

### Qualifications and certifications
- Part-qualified or qualified: ACCA, CIMA, CA Sri Lanka (CASL), AAT Sri Lanka, ICMA
- B.Com / BBA / BSc (Accounting or Finance) — University of Colombo, University of Sri Jayewardenepura, USJP, NSBM, ICBT, APIIT etc.
- Xero Certified Advisor badge (self-declared or verifiable via Xero)
- Diploma/certificate in Australian taxation or BAS agent-adjacent training is a strong (rare) bonus, not expected as standard

### Expected years of experience
- 2–6 years total accounting/bookkeeping experience
- Minimum 1–2 years specifically on Australian client ledgers/Xero files (this is the differentiator vs. general bookkeeping experience)

### Essential technical and communication capabilities
- Daily/weekly bank reconciliation and transaction coding in Xero
- Accounts payable: invoice capture, bill entry, payment runs
- Accounts receivable: invoicing, statement runs, debtor follow-up
- Month-end close tasks: accruals, prepayments, reconciliations, basic reporting packs
- Working knowledge of GST and BAS lodgement cycle (even if AU tax agent files it, candidate should understand coding implications)
- Fluent, professional written and spoken English suitable for direct client/debtor calls and emails — not just internal team English
- Comfort working AU business hours from Sri Lanka and following AU public holidays instead of SL holidays

### Indicators a candidate has directly supported Australian clients
- Explicit mention of "Australian client(s)", "AU book(s)", "ANZ", specific AU accounting terms (BAS, GST, ABN, single touch payroll/STP, superannuation) in their CV/profile
- Job title or employer clearly BPO/offshore delivery for Australia (e.g., "Client Accountant – Australia Portfolio")
- Xero + AU-specific integrations mentioned: Hubdoc, Dext/Receipt Bank, ApprovalMax, Deputy, Employment Hero/KeyPay
- Reference to working AU hours/shift or "night shift"/"AU shift" in profile or CV
- LinkedIn recommendations or endorsements from Australia-based colleagues/managers
- Mentions of AU tax agent liaison, AU public holiday calendar, or AU-based reporting deadlines

## 4. Automated Sourcing Workflow

| # | Stage | What happens | Automation role | Human checkpoint |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Candidate discovery | Run saved Boolean searches across LinkedIn Recruiter, job boards, Xero Advisor Directory on a schedule | Scheduled search execution (LinkedIn Recruiter Projects, ATS agent, or Make/Zapier-triggered pull from job board APIs where ToS-compliant) | Recruiter defines/approves search strings and sourcing channels |
| 2 | Profile/CV data extraction | Pull structured fields (title, employer, tenure, skills, education, certifications) from CV/profile into a standard record | AI-assisted parsing (ATS resume parser or LLM extraction against a fixed schema) | None required — extraction only, no decisions made |
| 3 | Duplicate detection | Match new records against existing candidate database by name + email/phone + LinkedIn URL | Automated fuzzy-match rule in ATS/spreadsheet (e.g., Power Automate/Make dedup step) | Recruiter resolves ambiguous/uncertain matches |
| 4 | Mandatory-requirement validation | Check CV/profile text for explicit evidence of each mandatory criterion; mark unmet/unclear as "Requires Validation" | AI checklist pass against job description (see Section 6 prompt) | Recruiter confirms before any candidate is excluded from pipeline |
| 5 | Candidate scoring | Apply weighted scorecard (Section 5) using only explicit evidence | AI scoring engine outputs score + evidence citations | Mandatory — no score is final until recruiter reviews |
| 6 | Human recruiter review | Recruiter reviews AI score, evidence, and flags for every candidate reaching the review threshold | — | **This step is mandatory for every profile, shortlisted or not** |
| 7 | Personalised outreach drafting | Draft LinkedIn/email message referencing candidate's actual background | AI drafts using Section 7 templates + candidate-specific details | Recruiter reviews/edits and manually approves send |
| 8 | Response tracking | Log opens, replies, and sentiment (interested/not interested/no response) | Automated logging via LinkedIn Recruiter inbox + email tracking (Power Automate/Make) into ATS/sheet | Recruiter interprets ambiguous responses |
| 9 | Follow-up reminders | Trigger reminder tasks at defined intervals if no response | Scheduled workflow (Power Automate/Make/Zapier) creates task/notification | Recruiter decides whether/how to follow up |
| 10 | ATS/spreadsheet updates | Update candidate stage, score, notes, outreach history | Automated field updates on status change | Recruiter manually updates stage on decisions (shortlist/reject) |
| 11 | Reporting dashboard | Aggregate pipeline metrics in real time | Automated refresh (Power BI/Google Sheets/Looker Studio linked to ATS) | Recruiter/TA lead reviews weekly |

**Non-negotiable rule embedded in the workflow:** stages 5–6 always run together — a score is never the basis for outreach or rejection until a recruiter has reviewed the evidence behind it.

## 5. Candidate Screening Scorecard (out of 100)

| Criterion | Weight | Type | What "evidence" looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Australian accounting experience | 15 | Mandatory | Explicit mention of AU clients, AU accounting terms, AU employer with ANZ scope |
| Xero proficiency | 15 | Mandatory | Daily/practical Xero use stated; certification is a bonus, not a requirement |
| End-to-end bookkeeping experience | 10 | Mandatory | Full-cycle bookkeeping (not just AP or just AR) explicitly described |
| Month-end & management reporting experience | 10 | Mandatory (month-end) / Desirable (mgmt reporting) | Accruals, prepayments, reconciliations, reporting packs mentioned |
| GST and BAS knowledge | 10 | Mandatory | Direct mention of GST coding, BAS prep/support, or working with a BAS agent/tax agent |
| Accounts payable & receivable experience | 10 | Mandatory | Invoice processing, payment runs, invoicing, statement runs, debtor follow-up |
| Client and debtor communication | 10 | Mandatory | Evidence of direct client/supplier/debtor-facing communication (not purely back-office) |
| Manufacturing, printing, freight, or job-costing exposure | 5 | Desirable | Job costing, inventory, freight reconciliation, or relevant industry employer |
| English communication (written/spoken) | 10 | Mandatory | Quality of CV/profile writing; verified further at interview — flag if unclear |
| Commercial judgement / accountability / attention to detail | 3 | Mandatory (qualitative) | Ownership language, initiative examples, accuracy-focused achievements |
| Availability (hybrid + AU shift + AU leave calendar) | 2 | Mandatory | Confirmed via screening question, not inferred from CV |
| **Total** | **100** | | |

### Mandatory vs. desirable
- **Mandatory:** Australian accounting exposure, Xero proficiency, end-to-end bookkeeping, month-end experience, GST/BAS knowledge, AP/AR experience, client/debtor communication, English communication, commercial judgement, shift/hybrid/leave-calendar availability, qualified/part-qualified accountant status.
- **Desirable:** manufacturing/printing/freight/job-costing exposure, prior BPO/Australian-firm employer, management reporting/margin analysis exposure, Xero certification.

### Knockout questions (asked at screening, never auto-decided)
1. Are you willing and able to work 6:30 AM–3:30 PM IST as your regular shift?
2. Are you able to follow the Australian public holiday/leave calendar rather than the Sri Lankan calendar?
3. Are you able to work in a hybrid model (specify office attendance expectation)?
4. Do you have direct, hands-on Xero experience (not just exposure)?

A "no" on a knockout question is flagged **"Recommended Reject – Pending Recruiter Confirmation,"** never auto-removed from the pipeline.

### Score thresholds
| Score range | Action |
|---|---|
| 70–100 | Shortlist consideration (still requires recruiter review and mandatory criteria all met) |
| 50–69 | Recruiter review queue — borderline, second look for transferable strengths |
| Below 50 | Deprioritised but retained in database and visible to recruiter; not auto-purged or auto-rejected |

### Areas that cannot be accurately assessed without a human interview
- True spoken English fluency and phone/video presence with AU clients
- Real-time debtor negotiation/de-escalation skill
- Depth of GST/BAS understanding vs. surface familiarity
- Cultural fit, adaptability to AU working style, and resilience under AU-hours pressure
- Commercial judgement in practice (only inferable from CV, not provable)

## 6. AI Candidate-Matching Prompt (reusable)

```
You are assisting a Talent Acquisition recruiter by comparing a candidate's CV/LinkedIn profile
against the Job Description for "Finance and Operations Accountant" at Swivel Group.

STRICT RULES:
- Only use information explicitly stated in the candidate's profile/CV. Do not assume, infer, or
  guess experience, skills, seniority, or personality traits that are not written.
- Do not consider or comment on age, gender, ethnicity, religion, marital status, photographs,
  name origin, or any other protected/irrelevant personal characteristic.
- If information needed for a criterion is missing or ambiguous, label it "Requires Validation" —
  do not penalise silently or assume the worst or best case.
- You must not issue a final reject decision. Your output is an input to a human recruiter's
  decision, not a decision itself.

JOB REQUIREMENTS TO ASSESS AGAINST:
[Insert full job description here]

CANDIDATE PROFILE/CV:
[Insert candidate CV/profile text here]

OUTPUT FORMAT:
1. Overall Match Score: X/100 (using the approved weighted scorecard categories)
2. Evidence Table: for each scorecard category, state the score awarded and quote/paraphrase the
   exact evidence from the CV/profile that supports it. If no evidence exists, write "No evidence
   found — Requires Validation."
3. Mandatory Requirements — Met: [list]
4. Mandatory Requirements — Not Met or Unclear: [list, each marked Requires Validation if unclear
   rather than assumed unmet]
5. Missing or Unclear Information: [list specific gaps a recruiter should ask about]
6. Potential Concerns: [factual, evidence-based only — e.g. "no AP/AR detail provided", never
   speculative personal judgments]
7. Recommended Screening Questions: [3–6 targeted questions to resolve the gaps above]
8. Final Recommendation: Strong Match / Possible Match / Not Suitable
   (this is a suggestion for the recruiter, not an automated decision)
```

## 8. Initial Screening Questions

1. How many years of hands-on bookkeeping experience do you have (end-to-end, not just one function)?
2. How would you rate your Xero proficiency, and what specific tasks do you regularly perform in Xero (e.g., bank reconciliation, invoicing, payroll, reporting)?
3. Have you directly supported an Australian business or Australian clients? In what capacity, and for how long?
4. What is your practical experience with Australian GST coding and/or BAS preparation/support?
5. What month-end tasks have you personally been responsible for (accruals, prepayments, reconciliations, reporting packs)?
6. Describe your experience with accounts payable, accounts receivable, bank reconciliation, and debtor follow-up.
7. Have you worked in a manufacturing, printing, freight, logistics, or job-costing environment? Please describe.
8. How confident are you communicating directly (phone/email/video) with clients, suppliers, or debtors in English? Can you give an example?
9. What is your current salary, and what is your expected salary for this role?
10. What is your current notice period?
11. Are you able to work in a hybrid arrangement (specify expected office days)?
12. Are you able to work the shift 6:30 AM–3:30 PM IST as your standard working hours?
13. Are you able to follow the Australian public holiday and leave calendar instead of the Sri Lankan calendar?

## 9. Automation Technology Recommendations

| Tier | Stack | What it covers | Implementation effort | Limitations / risks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Low-cost** | LinkedIn (free/Recruiter Lite) + Google Sheets + manual AI evaluation (ChatGPT/Claude, copy-paste CV text) + Gmail for outreach | Manual search, manual scoring with AI assistance, manual tracking in a shared sheet | Low — 1–2 days to set up templates and sheet structure | No automated dedup or reminders; relies on recruiter discipline; scales poorly beyond ~30–50 active candidates |
| **Intermediate** | LinkedIn Recruiter + ATS (e.g., Zoho Recruit, Manatal, Bullhorn) + Power Automate or Make for reminders/notifications + AI CV scoring via ATS integration or API call | Structured pipeline, automated stage tracking, scheduled follow-up reminders, dashboard views in ATS | Medium — 1–2 weeks incl. ATS configuration and workflow setup | Requires ATS licensing cost; integration/API setup needs some technical support; AI scoring still needs recruiter calibration |
| **Advanced / scalable** | LinkedIn Recruiter + full ATS with AI parsing + Make/Zapier/Power Automate orchestrating multi-step workflows (discovery alerts → parsing → dedup → scoring → task creation) + Power BI/Looker Studio dashboard + email sequencing tool (e.g., built-in ATS sequences) | End-to-end automated pipeline from discovery through reporting, with recruiter approval gates at each decision point | High — 3–5 weeks incl. testing, compliance review, and recruiter training | Higher cost; requires ongoing maintenance and monitoring of automation rules; greatest risk of over-automation if approval gates are weakened — must be actively governed to keep human review mandatory |

**Recommendation for immediate start:** begin with the Intermediate tier — an ATS with AI-assisted scoring plus a lightweight automation tool for reminders gives real efficiency gains without the governance overhead of a fully orchestrated pipeline. Move to Advanced once volume justifies it (e.g., multiple concurrent finance roles).

## 10. Recruitment Dashboard

| Field / Metric | Definition | Stage tracked |
|---|---|---|
| Candidates sourced | Total unique candidates identified across all channels | Discovery |
| Candidates meeting mandatory criteria | Count passing mandatory-requirement validation | Validation |
| Candidates reviewed | Count with completed recruiter review of AI score/evidence | Review |
| Outreach sent | Count of personalised messages sent (recruiter-approved) | Outreach |
| Response rate | Outreach sent ÷ responses received | Outreach |
| Positive response rate | Interested responses ÷ total responses | Outreach |
| Screening completion | Candidates who completed initial screening questions/call | Screening |
| Shortlisted candidates | Candidates confirmed by recruiter as shortlisted | Shortlist |
| Interview conversion rate | Shortlisted ÷ progressed to client/hiring-manager interview | Interview |
| Source effectiveness | Shortlist rate broken down by sourcing channel (LinkedIn, topjobs.lk, referral, etc.) | Reporting |
| Common skill gaps | Aggregated "Requires Validation" / unmet mandatory-criteria themes across candidate pool | Reporting |
| Time per stage | Average days spent in discovery, review, outreach, screening, and interview stages | Reporting |

Recommended refresh cadence: weekly review by the TA team, with source-effectiveness and skill-gap analysis reviewed monthly to refine sourcing strings and target companies.

## Safeguards (apply across every stage above)

- No candidate is automatically rejected by AI — every rejection is a recruiter decision.
- No inference of protected or sensitive characteristics (age, gender, ethnicity, religion, marital status, photographs, name origin, etc.).
- No scoring based on the irrelevant personal attributes above.
- Every score must cite evidence from the candidate's own profile/CV.
- Missing information is labelled "Requires Validation," never assumed positive or negative.
- Every shortlisted *and* rejected profile is reviewed by a recruiter before the decision is finalised.
- Sourcing uses only platform-compliant methods — no unauthorised scraping, no mass/spam messaging, no ToS-violating automation.
- Candidate data is stored in the ATS/sheet with access limited to authorised recruitment personnel only.

## Step-by-Step Implementation Plan for the TA Team

1. **Week 1 — Setup:** Finalise job description wording, confirm mandatory vs. desirable criteria with the hiring manager, set up ATS/spreadsheet fields matching the scorecard, load the Boolean strings into LinkedIn Recruiter Projects.
2. **Week 1 — Channel activation:** Post role on topjobs.lk and relevant channels; begin LinkedIn Recruiter searches; identify 10–15 target BPO/offshore/Xero-partner employers for direct sourcing.
3. **Week 1–2 — Discovery & extraction:** Run searches, pull candidate profiles/CVs into the ATS, run AI extraction against the standard schema, run duplicate detection.
4. **Week 2 — Validation & scoring:** Run the AI matching prompt (Section 6) against each candidate, populate the scorecard, flag mandatory gaps as "Requires Validation."
5. **Week 2 — Recruiter review:** Recruiter reviews every scored candidate (not just high scorers), confirms or overrides AI flags, decides review-queue vs. shortlist-consideration vs. deprioritised.
6. **Week 2–3 — Outreach:** AI drafts personalised messages for recruiter-approved candidates only; recruiter edits and sends; log all outreach in ATS.
7. **Week 3 — Response handling & screening:** Track responses; send screening questions (Section 8) to interested candidates; schedule screening calls.
8. **Week 3–4 — Follow-ups:** Automated reminders trigger recruiter follow-up on non-responders at defined intervals (e.g., day 4, day 8, day 12).
9. **Ongoing — Shortlist & handover:** Recruiter finalises shortlist with completed scorecards and screening notes; hand over to hiring manager for interviews.
10. **Weekly — Dashboard review:** TA lead reviews pipeline dashboard, adjusts sourcing channels/strings based on source-effectiveness and skill-gap data.
